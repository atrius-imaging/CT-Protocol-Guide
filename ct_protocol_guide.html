⚠ Oral Contrast Per Criteria (excludes CT Enterography)
Non-STAT — give if ANY apply:
  • BMI < 25
  • Oncology patient (if can tolerate)
  • Recent abdominal surgery (within past 30 days)
  • Known IBD (Crohn’s / UC)
  • Post gastric bypass (sip slowly)
  • Weight loss indication
STAT — give if ANY apply:
  • BMI < 25
  • Recent abdominal surgery
  • Known IBD
  • Post gastric bypass
Readi-Cat: contraindicated if suspected GI perforation or barium allergy — give Omnipaque Solution instead. Berry smoothie: no strawberry allergy. Vanilla smoothie: no gluten allergy.
⚡ IV Contrast (I+) Guidelines
Contraindications:
  • Prior contrast allergy — requires pre-medication
  • Prior anaphylaxis to contrast — scan WITHOUT contrast
  • eGFR < 30 — hold contrast, notify radiologist
Pre-medication (prior contrast allergy):
The following steroid prep is prescribed by the ordering clinician:
  • Methylprednisolone PO — 2 doses of 32 mg at 12 hrs and 2 hrs before exam
  • OR Prednisone PO — 3 doses of 50 mg at 13 hrs, 7 hrs, and 1 hr before exam
For history of prior steroid break-through:
  • ADD Diphenhydramine (Benadryl) 50 mg PO or IM 1 hr before exam
  • Patients receiving Benadryl MUST have someone to accompany them home.
For accelerated allergy premedication refer to: CMAI31
📋 Creatinine / eGFR Labs — order if patient has ANY:
  • Diabetic
  • History of kidney disease
  • Creatinine on file is more than 30 days old
👤 Pediatric Age Guidelines:
AgeCT
