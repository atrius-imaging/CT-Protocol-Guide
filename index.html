⚠ Oral Contrast Criteria
Non-STAT — give if ANY apply:
  • BMI < 25
  • Oncology patient (if can tolerate)
  • Recent abdominal surgery (within past 30 days)
  • Known IBD (Crohn’s / UC)
  • Post gastric bypass (sip slowly)
  • Weight loss indication
STAT — give if ANY apply:
  • BMI < 25
  • Recent abdominal surgery
  • Known IBD
  • Post gastric bypass
Readi-Cat: contraindicated if suspected GI perforation or barium allergy. Berry smoothie: no strawberry allergy. Vanilla smoothie: no gluten allergy.
📋 Creatinine / eGFR Labs — order if patient has ANY:
  • Diabetic
  • History of kidney disease
  • Creatinine on file is more than 30 days old
